US Job Market Visualizer GitHub

This is a research tool visualizing 342 occupations from the Bureau of Labor Statistics, covering 143M jobs. Each rectangle's area = total employment. Color = selected metric. This fork adds 4 scoring axes (digital AI exposure, physical/robotics exposure, adoption friction, demand elasticity) across 3 time horizons (2028, 2031, 2036), with a composite employment impact score. Drag the timeline slider to watch the treemap shift over time.
