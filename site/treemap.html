US Job Market Visualizer

A research tool visualizing 342 occupations from the Bureau of Labor Statistics, covering 143M jobs. Each rectangle's area = total employment. Color = selected metric. Scored across 4 axes (digital AI exposure, physical/robotics exposure, adoption friction, demand elasticity) at 3 time horizons (2028, 2031, 2036).

Horizon

Metric

⚠
These horizons represent when disruption arrives, not when the technology is invented. AI capabilities typically exist 2–4 years before they cause widespread employment impact. The technology behind the 2028 scores largely exists today — it simply hasn't fully diffused through procurement cycles, regulation, retraining, and institutional inertia. The 2031 scores reflect technology that exists now or will by ~2027, filtered through adoption friction. This means the disruption people will feel in 2031 is already baked in — policymakers looking at current employment figures are seeing the past, not the present.

Each occupation is scored 0–10 on the selected metric. 0 = no exposure or impact; 10 = the role is fully automatable or has ceased to exist in its traditional form. The composite score combines digital and physical AI exposure, modulated by adoption friction and demand elasticity.
